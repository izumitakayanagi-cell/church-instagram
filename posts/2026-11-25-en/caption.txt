Verse of the Week: Matthew 11:28 🌿

Midweek can feel heavy. Crowded trains, long to-do lists, and quiet worries we carry with us through the busy streets of Tokyo.

In this Bible verse, Jesus doesn't begin with a demand or a checklist. He begins with an invitation. The rest he offers isn't something we earn by finishing everything first. It's something we receive by turning to him, just as we are, tired and unfinished.

Maybe today you could try a small pause. Before your next meeting, or on the walk home, take one slow breath and hand over what's weighing on you in a simple prayer.

You don't have to carry it all alone.

If you'd like a calm place to rest and reflect, you're warmly welcome at our church's English service in Takadanobaba this Sunday. ☕
