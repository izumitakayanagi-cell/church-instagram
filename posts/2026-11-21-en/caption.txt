Tomorrow is Sunday, and we would love to welcome you to our English Service in Takadanobaba 🌿

This week we are reflecting on John 3:16, perhaps one of the most well-known verses in the Bible. It speaks of a love that is wide enough for the whole world, and personal enough for each one of us. Wherever you come from and whatever this week has held, you are included in that love.

If you are new to Tokyo, or have never been to church before, please don't worry. There is nothing you need to know or prepare. You are welcome to simply come, sit, listen, and rest for a while. Come as you are.

Life in a big city like Tokyo can feel busy, and sometimes a little lonely. We hope our church can be a quiet, friendly place to pause, open the Bible together, and meet others along the way.

We look forward to seeing you tomorrow ☺️

📖 John 3:16 (ESV)
"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
