Tomorrow is Sunday, and we would love to welcome you to our English Service here in Takadanobaba, Tokyo 🌿

This week's verse is John 3:16, perhaps one of the most familiar lines in the whole Bible. Familiar as it is, it still holds something quietly astonishing: a love that is wide enough to include the whole world, and personal enough to include you.

If you are new to Tokyo, still finding your way in a big city, or have never stepped inside a church before, please know you are very welcome just as you are. There is nothing you need to prepare or know in advance. You are free to simply come, sit, listen, and take your time.

City life can feel busy and sometimes a little lonely. We hope our small church can be a calm place to pause, reflect on God's love, and meet some friendly faces.

We look forward to seeing you tomorrow ☺️

📖 John 3:16 (ESV)
"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
