Midweek, it's easy to feel like everything depends on us 🌿

This week's Bible verse is Psalm 23:1, one of the most loved lines in all of Scripture. David, who once cared for sheep himself, describes God as a shepherd.

A shepherd doesn't wait for the sheep to figure everything out. He leads, protects and provides, often before the sheep even notice what they need. To "not want" isn't about having everything we wish for. It's the quiet confidence that we are cared for, and that we are not alone.

Life in Tokyo can be full and fast: crowded trains, long workdays, maybe family far away. If you feel stretched thin today, this verse offers a gentle reminder that you are known and looked after, even in the busiest city.

Perhaps take a moment today to slow down, breathe, and rest in that care 🙏
