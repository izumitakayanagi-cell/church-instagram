Yesterday, at our Harvest Thanksgiving Service, we heard God's Word from Colossians 3:15–17 in a message titled "People with a Thankful Heart" 🍂

In Japanese, "thank you" (arigatō) comes from arigatai — "something rare."
When we realize that each ordinary day we took for granted is actually a gift from God, gratitude is born.

Thankfulness is not a one-time feeling. As we reflect on the Lord's grace, we are gradually changed into people who give thanks — that was the message.

This week, may gratitude overflow in your words to those around you and in the small work of your hands.
