Tomorrow is Sunday, and we would love to welcome you to our English Service here in Takadanobaba, Tokyo. 🌿

This week we are reflecting on Isaiah 41:10, a gentle reminder from the Bible that we are not alone, even when life feels uncertain or overwhelming.

If you are new to Tokyo, starting over in a new city can be exciting, but it can also feel lonely at times. And if you have never been to church before, it is completely okay to come just as you are. There is no need to know the songs, bring anything, or have it all figured out.

You are welcome to sit, listen, and simply rest for a while. Our church family is happy to say hello, and just as happy to give you space if you prefer.

Whatever this week has held for you, we hope tomorrow can be a quiet moment of peace and hope. We look forward to seeing you. ☕

📖 Isaiah 41:10 (ESV)
"Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand."
